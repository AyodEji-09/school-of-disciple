## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the academic gravity, spiritual dignity, and quiet reverence of a premier ecclesiastical institution. Designed for the School of Disciples, it evokes the quiet stillness of an archival seminary library illuminated by warm morning light. The visual language balances ministerial authority with deep communal warmth.

### Design Movement
**Classical Academic Modernism:** The interface rejects fleeting digital trends—eschewing glassmorphism, iridescent gradients, and hyper-saturated hues. Instead, it relies on disciplined structural grids, warm tactile surfaces, literary serif headings, and purposeful geometric body typography. It prizes legible hierarchy, structural clarity, and quiet craftsmanship, creating an atmosphere that feels enduring, contemplative, and distinguished.

### Target Audience & Emotional Tone
- **Audience:** Disciple students, church leaders, faculty mentors, and institutional administrators across the Americas.
- **Atmosphere:** Scholarly, reverent, ordered, and profoundly welcoming. Every screen should feel as deliberate, calm, and legible as an illuminated folio or open study table.

## Colors

The palette is derived from collegiate dignity and morning illumination, ensuring strict WCAG AA contrast across all reading and interaction modes.

### Roles & Intent
- **Primary (`#1B2A5C` - Deep Navy):** Represents foundational authority, permanence, and theological rigor. Used for primary interactive triggers, active navigation items, prominent badges, and dominant headers.
- **Secondary (`#C99A2E` - Warm Gold):** Captures morning study light, honor, and academic milestones. Reserved for highlights, active state indicators, key calls-to-action, badges of distinction, and subtle structural accents. Avoid using as small text on white backgrounds.
- **Neutral Surface (`#FAF7F2` - Soft Ivory):** The primary canvas background, creating a comfortable, paper-like warmth that reduces eye strain compared to stark white.
- **Elevated Canvas (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** Used strictly for elevated cards, study modules, modal sheets, and reading trays.
- **Neutral Foreground (`#2B2F3A` - Charcoal):** Deep, legible tone for primary body text, titles, and high-emphasis labels.
- **Muted Structural (`#5B6170` - Slate Gray):** Secondary metadata, timestamps, disabled iconography, and subdued contextual descriptions.
- **Surface Borders (`#E4E1DA` - Mist Gray):** Structural dividing rules, card borders, and unselected component boundaries.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the editorial discipline of **Source Serif 4** with the functional clarity of **DM Sans**.

- **Editorial Serifs (Source Serif 4):** Reserved for course headers, discourse titles, theological citations, and section headings. Weights are strictly kept to 600 (SemiBold) and 700 (Bold) to project authority and archival depth without fragility.
- **Sans-Serif Workhorse (DM Sans):** Powers all pedagogical readings, syllabus bodies, interactive controls, inputs, and analytical dashboards. The neutral geometry guarantees effortless legibility on desktop and mobile viewports alike.
- **Typographic Discipline:** Avoid tracking out body text; keep `letterSpacing` tight and natural. Use uppercase styling sparingly, limited only to `label-sm` category chips or formal status rubrics.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model emphasizes academic order through a disciplined 12-column fluid grid system across desktop, scaling down to an 8-column system on tablet and a 4-column system on mobile devices.

### Structural Framework
- **Canvas Margins:** Section margins scale dynamically from generous desktop borders (`margin`: `2.5rem` / `40px`) to focused mobile viewports (`margin-mobile`: `1rem` / `16px`).
- **Column Gutters:** Desktop layout uses `1.5rem` (`24px`) to preserve breathing space between curriculum modules, reducing to `1rem` (`16px`) on handheld displays.
- **Max Content Container:** Standard content containers cap at `1200px` to maintain optimal line lengths for reading essays and lecture outlines, while data-heavy matriculation tables expand to a maximum container of `1440px`.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows an 8pt modular cadence. Layout distance tokens (`space-xs` through `space-xl`) determine component internal padding and visual gaps between neighboring elements.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on crisp surface layering and a singular, carefully tinted ambient shadow rather than heavy multi-tiered drops or glassy overlays.

### Principles
- **Base Canvas:** The foundation is always Soft Ivory (`#FAF7F2`), anchoring the layout in non-glare stillness.
- **Surface Elevation:** Interactive containers, cards, and reading plates are cast in Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), bounded by a delicate hairline border (`1px solid #E4E1DA`).
- **Signature Ambient Shadow:** Whenever physical lift is needed (e.g., elevated cards, active dropdowns, modal dialogs), apply exclusively:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px rgba(27, 42, 92, 0.08)`
  This shadow is tinted with Deep Navy (`#1B2A5C`) rather than sterile black, mirroring natural directional daylight and preventing synthetic gray cast.
- **Strict Prohibitions:** No skeuomorphic plastic bevels, no glossy highlights, no blurred glass containers, and no chromatic gradients. Depth is established purely through surface color shifts, crisp containment, and navy-tinted dissipation.

## Shapes

The design system employs a disciplined, balanced geometric approach where structural content containers maintain steady, grounded curvature while action elements utilize smooth pill geometry.

### Radius Assignments
- **Cards & Modules:** Fixed at `12px` (`0.75rem`) to create an approachable, book-jacket profile that feels structured yet welcoming.
- **Interactive Form Inputs:** Standardized at `10px` to offer subtle framing without feeling overly mechanical.
- **Buttons, Badges & Interactive Chips:** Strictly pill-shaped (`9999px` border radius, with buttons height-locked to `48px`) to clearly demarcate actionable controls from static content blocks.
- **Dialogs & Overlay Trays:** `16px` (`1rem`) to establish gentle visual precedence when floating above the base surface.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Height of `48px`, full pill radius (`9999px`), horizontal padding `space-lg` (`24px`). Background is Deep Navy (`#1B2A5C`) with Pure White text (`#FFFFFF`), typography set in `label-lg`. On hover, background deepens slightly while maintaining the signature shadow `0 4px 20px rgba(27, 42, 92, 0.08)`.
- **Secondary Button:** Height of `48px`, pill radius. Background is Warm Gold (`#C99A2E`) with Deep Navy (`#1B2A5C`) typography for maximum distinction during ceremonial or enrollment actions.
- **Tertiary / Outlined Button:** Height of `48px`, pill radius. Background transparent, border `1.5px solid #1B2A5C`, text in `#1B2A5C`.

### Cards & Study Panels
- Elevated surface in Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a `12px` border radius, bounded by a `1px solid #E4E1DA` hairline border and ambient shadow `0 4px 20px rgba(27, 42, 92, 0.08)`.
- Internal padding follows `space-lg` (`24px`) for compact study modules and `space-xl` (`32px`) for primary curriculum documents.
- Optional top accent rule: `3px` solid Warm Gold (`#C99A2E`) running along the upper border for featured modules or active terms.

### Input Fields
- Height of `48px`, border radius of `10px`. Background is Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), border is `1px solid #E4E1DA`.
- Typography is `body-md` in Charcoal (`#2B2F3A`), with placeholder text in Slate Gray (`#5B6170`).
- Focus state: Border shifts to Deep Navy (`#1B2A5C`) with a crisp `2px` focus ring tinted at `15%` opacity. No erratic animations.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Height of `28px`, full pill radius (`9999px`), padding `4px 12px`. Text set in `label-sm` (uppercase, bold tracking). Warm Gold tint (`#C99A2E` at 15% opacity with `#C99A2E` text) for distinction/honors, Mist Gray (`#E4E1DA` background with `#2B2F3A` text) for neutral cataloging.
- **Filter Chips:** Height of `36px`, pill radius, `1px solid #E4E1DA` border, Soft Ivory (`#FAF7F2`) background when idle. Active state transitions to Deep Navy (`#1B2A5C`) fill with Pure White text.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkboxes:** `20px × 20px` with a `4px` corner radius. Unchecked state is Pure White with a `1.5px solid #5B6170` border. Checked state fills with Deep Navy (`#1B2A5C`) displaying a sharp Pure White checkmark icon.
- **Radio Buttons:** `20px × 20px` circle. Active state displays a Deep Navy (`#1B2A5C`) outer border and an internal concentric Warm Gold (`#C99A2E`) dot of `8px`.

### Lists & Archival Tables
- List items feature a minimum touch height of `52px`, separated by hairline dividers (`1px solid #E4E1DA`).
- Table headers use Soft Ivory (`#FAF7F2`) backgrounds with `label-md` uppercase typography in Slate Gray (`#5B6170`).
- Hover states on archival records trigger a subtle surface shift to `#F5F2EB`.

### Scripture & Quote Callouts
- Pure White card featuring a left vertical accent border (`4px solid #C99A2E`), italicized `body-lg` in Source Serif 4, and attribution displayed in `label-md` DM Sans Charcoal (`#2B2F3A`).
